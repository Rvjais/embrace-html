Why Early Intervention Changes Everything

Research consistently shows that the earlier a child receives targeted support, the better their long-term outcomes. The brain is most adaptable in the first eight years of life — making early identification and intervention critical for children with developmental differences. At eMbrace, we use gold-standard tools to identify challenges early and create structured care plans that evolve with your child. Whether it is a two-year-old not yet speaking, a seven-year-old struggling at school, or a twelve-year-old overwhelmed by social situations — we intervene at the right time, in the right way. Every month of early support can save years of struggle later.

"Early intervention can improve outcomes by up to 80% — WHO, 2023"

Our Core Services

Two pillars of expert child support — deep assessment and targeted therapy.

Assessments

Gain deep insight into your child’s strengths and challenges with expert-led evaluations

  • Ages & Stages Questionnaire Tracks developmental milestones across age groups.
  • ADOS (Autism Diagnostic Observation Schedule) Gold-standard diagnostic tool for autism.
  • Parent Interview A comprehensive look at developmental history and behavioral patterns.
  • Mullen Scales of Early Learning Evaluates motor and cognitive skills in young children.
  • CARS (Childhood Autism Rating Scale) Behavior-based autism screening.
  • WISC (Wechsler Intelligence Scale for Children) Measures intelligence and cognitive ability.
  • WIAT (Wechsler Individual Achievement Test) Assesses reading, spelling, and arithmetic skills.
  • ADHD & LD Screening Evaluates attention, impulsivity, and learning differences.

Therapy & Interventions

Support your child’s growth with personalized, evidence-based therapies

  • Occupational Therapy Builds fine motor skills, sensory processing, and independence.
  • Speech & Language Therapy Enhances speech clarity, comprehension, and expression.
  • Behavior Therapy Helps children manage emotions and develop social-emotional skills.
  • Expressive Arts Therapy Uses art, music, and movement to support emotional well-being.
  • Physiotherapy Strengthens mobility, posture, and physical development.
  • Special Education Support Personalized learning strategies to support academic growth.
  • Early Intervention Programs Timely, structured support for children 0–6 years old.

Parent & Family Support

Guidance sessions for parents, home strategies, school liaison, and sibling support.

Because supporting your child starts with supporting you.

  • Parent Coaching Practical strategies to use at home every day.
  • School Liaison We communicate with teachers and school Counsellors to ensure consistent support.
  • Home Program Design Structured activities designed for your home environment.
  • Sibling Support Helping brothers and sisters understand and connect with their sibling.
  • Family Therapy Sessions Strengthening family dynamics and communication.
  • Resource Navigation Helping you access the right support systems outside eMbrace.

Our services are never one-size-fits-all. Every child who joins eMbrace begins with a comprehensive intake and standardised assessment process. From there, our multidisciplinary team — including psychologists, speech therapists, occupational therapists, behaviour therapists, and special educators — collaborates to build a care plan unique to your child\u2019s strengths, challenges, and goals. Progress is reviewed regularly, and plans are adjusted as your child grows.

What to Expect — For Kids & Parents

We know that therapy can feel unfamiliar — especially for children. Here's exactly how every step of the eMbrace journey works.

01

We start with listening

Your first session is a no-pressure conversation. Our care coordinator gathers your concerns, your child's history, and your goals — so the right specialist is matched from day one.

02

Gold-standard assessment

Using tools like ADOS-2, WISC, CARS, and clinical observation, our team builds a complete picture of your child's strengths and support areas. No rushed conclusions.

03

Your roadmap, explained clearly

We walk you through every finding without jargon. Co-created care plans, realistic timelines, and home strategies you can use right away. You're never left guessing.

04

Therapy begins — at your pace

Evidence-based sessions, online or in-person, at a frequency that works for your family. Progress is tracked and shared with you every 4–6 weeks.

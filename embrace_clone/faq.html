How can we help?

Find answers to some of our most frequently asked questions

You can book a session in three easy steps:

Visit Our Website – Go to www.embracelives.com and click on "Book a Consultation."

Choose a Service – Select the type of session you need (individual therapy, assessment, couples therapy, etc.).

Schedule & Pay – Pick a therapist, select a time slot, and complete the payment securely.

Alternatively, you can book via WhatsApp or email at info@embracelives.com

We provide a range of evidence-based mental health and developmental services, including:

Individual Therapy – For adults, adolescents, and children

Psychometric & Developmental Assessments

Couples & Relationship Therapy

Parental Coaching & Support

Psychiatric Consultation & Medication Management

Corporate & School-Based Mental Health Programs

If you or someone you know is experiencing a mental health emergency, please seek immediate professional help.

Call a crisis helpline (India: Snehi Helpline - 1860 266 2345 | iCall - +91 9582208181)

Visit the nearest hospital or emergency care center.

Reach out to a trusted family member or friend for support.

eMbrace is not a crisis intervention platform. If you’re in immediate distress, please seek in-person psychiatric assistance.

While our therapists support individuals dealing with emotional distress, we do not offer emergency suicide prevention services.

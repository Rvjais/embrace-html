Disclaimer

www.embracelives.com  is not equipped to handle medical or psychological emergencies. Our services are not intended for crisis situations, which may include, but are not limited to, thoughts of self-harm, suicidal ideation, or symptoms of severe clinical disorders such as schizophrenia and other psychotic conditions. In such instances, immediate in-person medical attention is the most suitable form of assistance.
If you find yourself facing any of these challenges, or if you are contemplating suicide or feel that you pose a risk to yourself or others, we implore you to seek immediate help. Please call at the mentioned helplines numbers or proceed to the nearest hospital or emergency room to connect with medical professionals like psychiatrists, social workers, counselors, or therapists face-to-face. In the event of any medical or psychological health emergency, it is also advisable to involve a trusted family member or friend who can provide support during this critical time. Remember, your safety is paramount, and professional in-person support is essential in an emergency.

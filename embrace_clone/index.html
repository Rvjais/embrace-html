About eMbrace

Delhi's Most Trusted Psychology & Mental Health Practice

Founded by Dr. Supriya Malik, eMbrace is a premier mental health and neurodevelopmental care centre headquartered in Delhi. As one of the leading teams of psychologists in Delhi, we bring together RCI-certified clinical psychologists, speech-language pathologists, occupational therapists,child psychologists, physiotherapists, expressive arts therapists and special educators — all under one roof.

Whether you're looking for a child psychologist in Delhi for early developmental support or a clinical psychologist in Delhi for adult therapy and assessments, our internationally trained team delivers gold-standard, evidence-based care with warmth and precision.

We partner with embassies, international schools, hospitals, and leading corporates — serving families across India and abroad through both online and in-person sessions.

Conditions We Treat

Our clinical psychologists in Delhi are trained to assess and treat a wide range of psychological, neurodevelopmental, and emotional conditions across all age groups & child.

Anxiety & Stress

Evidence-based CBT and counselling for anxiety disorders, panic attacks, and chronic stress.

Depression

Compassionate therapy for depression, mood disorders, and emotional well-being recovery.

ADHD

Comprehensive ADHD assessments and management for children, adolescents, and adults.

Autism (ASD)

Standardized autism evaluations, early intervention, and individualized therapy programs.

Learning Disabilities

Psychoeducational assessments and remedial support for dyslexia, dyscalculia, and more.

OCD & Phobias

Specialized exposure therapy and CBT protocols for obsessive-compulsive and phobic disorders.

Relationship & Couples Therapy

Professional counselling for communication issues, conflict resolution, and relationship healing.

Trauma & PTSD

Trauma-informed care using internationally recognized therapeutic approaches for recovery.

Speech & Language Delays

Expert speech-language pathology for articulation, fluency, and communication development.

Behavioural Issues

Applied behaviour analysis and parent training for conduct challenges in children and teens.

Everything you need to know about finding the right psychologist in Delhi and starting your mental health journey.

At eMbrace, we match you with the right psychologist based on your specific concerns — whether it's anxiety, ADHD, autism, relationship issues, or child development. Our team of RCI-certified clinical psychologists in Delhi conduct a thorough initial consultation to understand your needs before recommending a personalised care plan.

eMbrace stands out for its internationally aligned, evidence-based approach. We use NICE protocols, Pearson-accredited assessment tools, and I-CAN approved programs. Our team includes MPhil and PhD-level clinical psychologists, speech therapists, and occupational therapists — offering comprehensive lifespan care under one roof.

Yes, we have a dedicated team of child psychologists in Delhi specializing in ADHD, autism spectrum disorder, learning disabilities, speech delays, behavioural challenges, and emotional regulation. We provide developmentally aligned assessments and therapy for children as young as 18 months through adolescence.

Absolutely. We offer both online and in-person sessions across all our services. Our online therapy is conducted through secure, HIPAA-compliant video platforms — giving you access to Delhi's top psychologists from anywhere in India or abroad.

Programs We Offer

Adult Mental Health Integration

  • Psychiatric Services: Evaluation, medication, long-term psychiatric support.
  • Individual Therapy: For anxiety, grief, trauma, burnout, and neurodivergence.
  • Staff Well-Being Programs: Workshops for doctors, nurses, and paramedical teams.

Pediatric & Adolescent Support

  • Therapy Services: OT, Speech, Special Education, Behavior Therapy on-site or hybrid.
  • Psychological Support: Therapy for anxiety, emotional regulation, social challenges.
  • Diagnostic Evaluations: Autism, ADHD, SLD—valid for school accommodations.

Early Intervention & NICU Programs

  • NICU Developmental Care: Screening & milestone tracking for high-risk infants.
  • Maternal Mental Health: Perinatal/postnatal therapy for emotional well-being.
  • Parent Guidance: Hospital-based coaching and early support planning.
